2. Double-click START_WEB3.bat in this folder.
3. It opens http://localhost:8003.
4. Keep the black server window open.

6. In Fairwork click Connect Wallet.
7. The badge must change from Demo Mode to Web3 Mode.

   0x37a55927e93f31Ba6dC7BE4f6628d115d93B88dF
