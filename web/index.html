HOW IT WORKS

Escrow lifecycle

1
Create

Client and freelancer agree on milestones.

2
Fund

Client deposits the milestone amount into escrow.

3
Submit

Freelancer adds a work link for review.

4
Approve

Client approves or requests changes.

5
Release

Approved payment is sent to the freelancer.

!
Dispute

Disputed funds remain locked until resolved.
