# Fairwork Demo Script

## 2-minute presentation flow

1. **Problem** — Freelancers and clients face payment and trust risk.
2. **Create project** — Brand Logo and Website Design.
3. **Milestones** — Logo Concepts, Homepage Design, Final Brand Kit.
4. **Escrow** — Client funds one milestone.
5. **Work submission** — Freelancer submits a link.
6. **Approval** — Client approves; escrow releases the milestone payment in the working flow.
7. **Dispute** — Show a disagreement on another milestone.
8. **Arbitration** — Three arbitrators vote Pay/Refund.
9. **Majority** — Two matching votes resolve the case.
10. **Outcome** — Refund Client or Pay Freelancer.

## One-line pitch

Fairwork is a blockchain-based freelance platform that uses milestone escrow and arbitration to reduce payment disputes between clients and freelancers.
